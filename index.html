Deepwell

The well has no bottom. Every floor is new, every potion color, wand wood, ring gem and fountain is a secret kept until you test it, the order of the depths is shuffled for your save, and each monster hides a weakness. Nothing carries over except what you write in your journal. Descend as far as you can.
